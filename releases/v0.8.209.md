# HyperFrames v0.8.209

Released on 2026-10-27.

Chrome now closes quickly in every managed browser, and audio effects launch it like other renders. Previews load project media and HDR clips the way render does.

- Phone videos with an undecodable first audio track check, preview and render ([cfef528d3ae1d](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/cfef528d3ae1d4549db648242e8f4c9cc7524d71), [#6551](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/6551)).
