# HyperFrames v0.8.206

Released on 2026-10-26.

Scrubbing a long film in Studio costs less, and a composition with no timeline script no longer waits 45 seconds before it renders. Preview and render also agree on the video frame and on a PNG colours, and HEIC photos now work in compositions.
