# HyperFrames v0.8.91

Released on 2026-09-29.

An app that embeds the timeline can now map a ruler click to the right time, even when the track-name column is open. Clips inside nested compositions sit at their master time, and moving one word moves only that word.

## Features

- **Studio:** The timeline publishes where it draws 0 s, so an embedding app can map a click to a time ([b171ee571](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/b171ee57169604f425082d48307ad9d0de18be5c), [#4724](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4724)).
