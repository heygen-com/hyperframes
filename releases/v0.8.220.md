# HyperFrames v0.8.220

Released on 2026-10-30.

Playback no longer stalls at a cut while the next clip has not started, which removes a stutter at every cut. Text-to-speech also finds its voice package when your shell sets a PYTHONPATH.

## Fixes

- **Core:** The playhead keeps moving at a cut while the incoming take has not started ([471d60a8a](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/471d60a8ac5f5a9824c5dde6c8d487bd0e436174), [#6768](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/6768)).
- **Core:** A still sub-composition's data-no-timeline on its template root skips the timeline wait ([c5a52037c](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/c5a52037c92), [#6762](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/6762)).
- **CLI:** Check flags caption words hidden behind a cut-out and ignores a mostly transparent cover ([f523ee2fc](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/f523ee2fcac), [#6770](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/6770)).
- **CLI:** Text-to-speech ignores a foreign PYTHONPATH when it looks for kokoro-onnx ([d1c059371](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/d1c0593717d50fe4cd374775f8519df22b6ed517), [#6769](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/6769)).
