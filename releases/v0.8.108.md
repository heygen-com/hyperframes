# HyperFrames v0.8.108

Released on 2026-10-02.

Studio gets four more audio parts: audible videos in preview, clip menu presets, loudness with voice ducking, and a waveform strip with peak marks. Undo now puts a moved element back at once, and a pick made just before a reload is kept.

## Features

- **Studio:** Waveform strip, clip light, peak marks and video beat source (5/8) ([824e4100c63](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/824e4100c636d30ac7c3036d9f5276c50e31eea4), [#4817](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4817)).
- **Studio:** Normalize loudness and duck under voice, with a limiter report (4/8) ([9244c47328e](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/9244c47328e41e59034bd9bbec293fe3f7310cf6), [#4816](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4816)).
- **Studio:** Speed presets, voice, look, crop presets and freeze frame in the clip menu (3/8) ([000ccd426a3](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/000ccd426a3ecf700eff6f449e9f5eab42d393fd), [#4815](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4815)).
- **Core:** Audible videos in preview web audio, audio groups, meters and carve (2/8) ([3c299acc7f6](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/3c299acc7f6fd7f9802e808c41a34d8593473549), [#4814](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4814)).

## Fixes

- **Studio:** Keep a pick made just before a reload swaps the preview frame ([c967a055fb8](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/c967a055fb8cd03ba4bd7cade6da8f5b12169f7b), [#4888](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4888)).
- **Studio:** Undo paints a move or nudge back at once while its save is still running ([880b4c41b3c](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/880b4c41b3c5708bd9a4317dab687c082219d3d6), [#4877](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4877)).
