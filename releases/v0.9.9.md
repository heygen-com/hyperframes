# HyperFrames v0.9.9

Released on 2026-11-03.

HyperFrames plan members can pull style templates with `hyperframes templates pull` and make films in them with the style-sampling skill. The CLI now installs skills that match its own release and no longer auto-updates across 0.x minor versions.

## Features

- **Engine:** A script-load failure names where the script came from and why it failed ([dec465949](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/dec465949b89a97084595ed336708c3e8ad6e3fa), [#7088](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/7088)).
- **Skills:** Style templates on the HyperFrames plan ([2daedcdaf](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/2daedcdafcb6857d04f7c5490d652057656a2129), [#6842](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/6842)).
