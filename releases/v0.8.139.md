# HyperFrames v0.8.139

Released on 2026-10-06.

Built-in transcription works again inside the HyperFrames desktop app, and `transcribe --json` now streams words as they are heard. Studio drags land where they were pressed, and renders keep a fromTo's starting values from its first frame.

## Features

- **CLI:** Stream heard words on the transcribe --json progress stream ([156e87766](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/156e87766c77c8b9c680b7dc107f94b4ee734f17), [#5127](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/5127)).
