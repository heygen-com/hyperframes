# HyperFrames v0.8.186

Released on 2026-10-22.

Capture motion blur now reaches renders and Studio, and it turns itself off, saying why, on pages it cannot blur safely. `hyperframes history` lists its entries even when it cannot write them, and `init --video` frames the project to the clip's shape.
