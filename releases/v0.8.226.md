# HyperFrames v0.8.226

Released on 2026-11-01.

Safari keeps edited films' audio playing, and renders can pass a lone full-frame video straight through when you opt in. Apps that host Studio can show their own preview loader.

## Features

- **Producer:** Opt-in video layer passthrough for a lone full-canvas video ([98520366a](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/98520366a6fccb0447a61f8e4baffa0f2568c359), [#6914](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/6914)).
- **Studio:** A host that draws its own loader can turn off both of Studio's preview loaders ([a7075fcb6](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/a7075fcb6c712e183736aac938d3ef57e8b12004), [#6911](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/6911)).
- **Core:** WebKit plays bare audio from a decoded buffer on the audio clock ([e181d34ed](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/e181d34edc45e2f9590007ebf7318f8a139bf747), [#6912](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/6912)).
- **Core:** The playback health reading says which clock the film followed ([bba3676a3](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/bba3676a3705e1e51a239899404f9982fca86951), [#6906](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/6906)).
