# HyperFrames v0.8.65

Released on 2026-09-23.

Studio edits feel lighter: changing one scene only redraws that scene's thumbnails, and timeline clips keep their pictures while you scroll instead of blanking out. The player no longer shows a stray "transition" label while assets load, and looping Lottie animations in the catalog play smoothly from the start.
