# HyperFrames v0.8.99

Released on 2026-09-30.

Manual editing in Studio is more exact. Dragged and rotated elements follow the pointer and land where you drop them. Resizing a cropped element keeps the same part in view, and moving a clip on the timeline moves its animations too.

## Fixes

- **Studio:** A dragged or rotated element follows the pointer exactly and keeps its sub-pixel spot on drop ([ca3a3f6e0](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/ca3a3f6e0b37c993ec25a5280bd74fbfc828c0db), [#4766](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4766)).
- **Core:** Pair a staged scene copy's video with its own render frame ([7b0ec6970](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/7b0ec697040083a9b92c0b4fb87abdb5f6774bc7), [#4791](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4791)).
- **Parsers:** Moving a clip on the timeline moves its inner animations too ([f1c1f1d69](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/f1c1f1d69cfe3a6b1c2d80bc88043e26fd52b907), [#4761](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4761)).
- Readers never see an empty or half-written project file ([567525a8c](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/567525a8c2e420c8590f7858d522bd13a20c0783), [#4777](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4777)).
- **Studio:** Resizing a cropped element keeps the same part of it in view ([76c634dfa](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/76c634dfa5f9d0119342e0eda80325aee518dff4), [#4743](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4743)).
