# HyperFrames v0.8.103

Released on 2026-10-01.

Fade handles in Studio are easier to grab and show a real tooltip. Layers positioned with min(), max() or clamp() move from where they really are, and projects named with a colon or # open correctly.

## Fixes

- **Studio:** Fade handles show a real tooltip and a 24 px target ([e3ac225a9](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/e3ac225a9ce63a5dd54447d616878e3989a32701), [#4819](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4819)).
- **Studio:** A layer with a min(), max() or clamp() translate moves from where it really is ([7880d41bc](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/7880d41bc412672c908b14da8c77e40eddb508a7), [#4832](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4832)).
- **Studio:** Open projects named with a colon and keep preview assets for names with # ([a369708fa](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/a369708fa5ddd02dd538b676daa86c72b278c4eb), [#4829](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4829)).
