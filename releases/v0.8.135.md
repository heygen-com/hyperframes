# HyperFrames v0.8.135

Released on 2026-10-05.

Undo now takes back the last edit, even right after a resize or a nudge. The Layers panel shows each layer's kind with an icon, a nested scene follows its clip when the clip moves, and a render fails clearly when a composition's own script throws.

## Fixes

- **Studio:** Undo takes back the last edit, even right after a resize or a nudge ([98fe01169](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/98fe011699999901affa2f6aa558568d17dc9e1a), [#5048](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/5048)).
- **Studio:** Layers rows show a kind icon, and only a group row has a caret ([f7d673476](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/f7d6734761967860eaecd7466ba610ef9bef349b), [#5067](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/5067)).

- **Core:** A sub-composition follows its host clip when the host moves without a reload ([5d19e23cb](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/5d19e23cb4352cde0065d966e3ef3b5e2b955e4b), [#5058](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/5058)).
