# HyperFrames v0.9.10

Released on 2026-11-04.

`hyperframes billing` now shows the price and pays from the terminal on Enter, and `billing add-credits` replaces `billing top-up`. Templates pull no longer counts an empty folder or a stray file as an installed template, and media-use sends pictures without ffmpeg.

## Features

- **CLI:** Billing and upgrade links carry the signed-in workspace ([c5a5e0882](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/c5a5e08827d5b184a7b608ff50034a618c4d9829), [#7103](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/7103)).
- **CLI:** Billing top-up is renamed to add-credits with no alias ([d21877eb2](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/d21877eb20b0a22588fb6cd5c024438bafc0bb89), [#7121](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/7121)).
- **CLI:** Billing shows the price and pays from the terminal on Enter ([87a42e747](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/87a42e7470c28aa689934a28a0a53784459b99f2), [#7068](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/7068)).
