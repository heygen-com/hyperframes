# HyperFrames v0.8.87

Released on 2026-09-29.

An app that embeds Studio can now mount its canvas handles, save style edits and hide the audio effects controls. The player can loop a range of a film, `hyperframes clean` frees disk space, and sound effects start on time.

## Features

- **CLI:** `hyperframes clean` finds and removes what HyperFrames left on disk ([663b02297](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/663b02297d58b56d7153bb156451ebb2b830941b), [#4701](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4701)).
- **Studio:** An embedding app can mount Studio's move, resize and rotate handles over its own preview ([cc792d4a8](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/cc792d4a8a9e79cca0678f1be68b54a51ae59d81), [#4708](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4708)).
- **Player:** Play and loop a range of a film with range-start and range-end ([840f65702](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/840f657028b9cf7f20a1a2efb9d436e52aa42d71), [#4688](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4688)).
- **Studio:** An embedding app can hide the timeline's audio effects controls ([dc61ce105](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/dc61ce105bc5ead8d4c0262298bc2aff5031c233), [#4704](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4704)).
- **Studio:** An embedding app can save style edits on a selection through Studio's own style save ([0af671c6d](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/0af671c6df8f8f740f2e982247ae3eab67dd0514), [#4698](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4698)).
