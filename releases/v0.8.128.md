# HyperFrames v0.8.128

Released on 2026-10-05.

Splitting, converting or editing a tween no longer deletes keyframes Studio cannot read. Dragging or resizing many selected clips now saves in one write per file.

- **Core:** The renderer's font pipeline is importable from @hyperframes/core/fonts/embed ([b3d2b82fe](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/b3d2b82fe95542af84a856e3d6f4ac7b333b044b), [#5041](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/5041)).
