# HyperFrames v0.8.109

Released on 2026-10-02.

Renders dropdown options take a mouse click again, a text edited in place shows its caret in a scaled preview, and soft reloads now match the saved files. Studio also links a video to its audio and can repair clips that drift out of sync.
