# HyperFrames v0.8.229

Released on 2026-11-01.

Signing in from the CLI now waits up to 60 minutes, so finishing checkout during sign-in no longer ends on a connection error. Trimming a clip keeps its thumbnail strip in place, and masks hold up better on moving footage.
