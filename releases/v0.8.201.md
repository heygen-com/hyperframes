# HyperFrames v0.8.201

Released on 2026-10-26.

Studio no longer gets stuck on a leftover history lock, so chat and edits keep working after a restart. Renders on a Mac decode video faster, and hidden clips stay hidden in preview and render.

## Features

- **Studio:** Zoom the timeline to the selected clips ([d787681d12e89](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/d787681d12e89f8adc8123e66ab9dfa37e8dc86f), [#5824](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/5824)).
