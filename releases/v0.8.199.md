# HyperFrames v0.8.199

Released on 2026-10-25.

Long HDR videos now render without filling the disk, and parallel capture stays on after one failed frame check. Lint warns when a scene's styles sit outside its template.

## Features

- **Lint:** Warn when a scene's styles sit outside its template ([ad6771c435](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/ad6771c435e6fb57c540f630820d2a1265c0c63d), [#6317](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/6317)).
