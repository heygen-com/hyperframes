# HyperFrames v0.9.29

Released on 2026-11-10.

Undo no longer loses work caught in the middle of a save, and the CLI shows the credits an image actually cost. Fewer renders are wrongly refused as too slow, and every frame of a video clip shows its own source frame.

## Features

- **CLI:** Label GPT image prices as estimates and show the charged credits ([0ba894123](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/0ba8941235f7bb3b40e10bb79bd27130543718c3), [#7351](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/7351)).

## Fixes

- **Studio Server:** History waits out a file a save has emptied, and rereads one written mid-copy ([e8416b2a4](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/e8416b2a41e537ed2f0c56e66a8ce585d1fb5b01), [#7480](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/7480)).
- **Producer:** Refuse a too-slow render only when its typical frame says it cannot finish ([88db83092](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/88db83092a635b9e3ef2b7f90e2f50261e8e5cdb), [#7479](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/7479)).
- **Engine:** A video clip shows a new source frame on every frame it is on screen ([235c80bae](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/235c80bae0cde99754cab78609cb5d7d210b650e), [#7471](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/7471)).
