# HyperFrames v0.8.98

Released on 2026-09-30.

Manual editing in Studio is steadier. A click after an empty-canvas press now selects on the first try, and drags no longer stall on the font list. Moves in files without GSAP save right away, and hosts can add their own items to a clip’s right-click menu.
