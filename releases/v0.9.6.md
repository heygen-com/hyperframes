# HyperFrames v0.9.6

Released on 2026-11-02.

Audio stems can now be read through your HeyGen account, with no Gemini key. The player shows the blurred frame and the mark while assets load. Safari audio is ready at the cut, and the preview recovers its WebGL scenes after a lost context. Reverse start records a run and stops a Free account when the server blocks it. Cloud export converts media whose contents do not match its file extension, and frames the page reports unchanged skip their screenshot.

## Features

- **CLI:** `hyperframes audio --read` describes audio stems through your HeyGen account, with no Gemini key needed ([acd5c30e60](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/acd5c30e6060f641941b12607bdb913bbba4eb2d), [#6993](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/6993)).
- **CLI:** `reverse start` records a reverse run, and cloud render carries its session ([7d3c225207](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/7d3c225207b23aaa4ef3c0babc481a38a301dba5), [#7016](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/7016)).
- **CLI:** `reverse start` stops a Free account when the server blocks it ([7da583ec69](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/7da583ec6922fd8fd06f7b377bdcf740d96891ed), [#7017](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/7017)).
- **Player:** Loading assets shows the blurred frame and the mark, like Desktop ([e3a7cdd2b8](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/e3a7cdd2b87b447175ca4f0cb7f521c4369db7f4), [#6934](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/6934)).
