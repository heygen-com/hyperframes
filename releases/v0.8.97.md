# HyperFrames v0.8.97

Released on 2026-09-30.

Copied and duplicated clips in Studio keep their look and names, and undo, group selection and saved font weights now behave as expected. Hosts can also trim the timeline tool menu and point popovers at their triggers.

## Features

- **Studio:** Select rightward takes crossing clips, and a host can keep only Select and Split ([9a27b9f93](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/9a27b9f9349b43b9fd194a95ace830d7cbe38b42), [#4755](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4755)).
- **Studio:** A popover can point at its trigger with an optional arrow ([18a1b226c](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/18a1b226c5e67e12308ee82afd7a5be5834df39a), [#4758](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4758)).
- **Studio:** Let a host make the timeline read-only while seek, scroll and zoom stay live ([ae914334f](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/ae914334f279ee53b3aebd0437ef7a9c4d20f8b0), [#4749](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/4749)).
