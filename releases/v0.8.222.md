# HyperFrames v0.8.222

Released on 2026-10-30.

Render takes --start and --end to make just one span of the film as its own MP4. Capture retries without WebGL when a page stops answering, so more exports finish. Lint warns when a font or image in a linked stylesheet points at the wrong folder.
