# HyperFrames v0.8.194

Released on 2026-10-25.

Undo and restore in Studio keep the media they delete or replace, so a history step never loses a file. Motion blur no longer blacks out videos inside sub-compositions, and around 25 smaller fixes land across the CLI, core and lint.
