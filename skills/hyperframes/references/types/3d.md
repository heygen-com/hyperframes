# 3D, simulations and generative pieces

Use 3D when the film earns it: an object or a set as the payoff, a product, a simulation that explains. Loading Three.js, WebGPU, p5 or canvas and the seek protocol are in `../runtimes.md`.

## Deterministic by construction

- Draw every frame from timeline time in the `hf-seek` listener; frames render out of order.
- Simulate once at load (physics, agents, particles) into arrays, then look up the state at `t`: `render(t)` stays a pure function. Heavy setup (meshes, shader compiles, the simulation) registers a `window.__hf.buildReady` promise.
- Seed every random choice; a seeded hash of the index, never `Math.random()`.

## Camera and story

- One hero object beats a crowd. Fly through or into it rather than orbiting it: one dive is worth ten turns.
- Cutaways, exploded views and chapters with one caption line each, plus labels with real numbers, turn a simulation into an explanation instead of eye candy.
- Product UI is never tilted into 3D; only generated content tilts.

## Light and materials

- Light it with a warm key, a cool rim and a dark environment with two or three soft strips; turn the environment, not the object, so highlights sweep across it; a contact shadow under the object.
- Bloom eats detail: keep it to the brightest points. Glossy metal under a low light gives white streaks: roughness about 0.45 or more, environment intensity about 0.5.
- One NaN pixel turns a whole frame black through bloom: clamp values that can divide by zero.

## Check it

Snapshot the dive and the payoff (`../review.md`); a frame that takes too long to draw slows every render, so profile the heaviest moment once.
