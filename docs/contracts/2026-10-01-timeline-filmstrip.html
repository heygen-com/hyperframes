Decision before implementation

1. Which band colour is intended?

The chosen prototype's Filmstrip band is dark. The brief calls it coloured. This changes the pixels that ship.

Prototype FilmstripDark band, undimmed frames. The prototype's 0.8 dim value affects Ribbon only. Its audio clips keep a 21px radius.

What would prove this page wrong

  • A running fixture whose name overlaps its picture after reserving the band at the clip wrapper.
  • A host build still painting 8px corners or its existing 2px plus 3px selection ring after the Studio release. This would expose a missed override.
  • A portrait or landscape composition still letterboxed after the composition thumbnail uses cover.
