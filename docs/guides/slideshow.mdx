## Make it

Give the agent the material and the decision the presentation should support:

```text
Turn this product strategy outline into a presentation for our leadership review: [paste outline]
```

The agent confirms the audience, the main outcome, and
whether the deck needs reveals, branches or presenter notes; you don't need to plan every slide. With your own agent instead of the app, start the message with `/hyperframes`.

Name the audience and what should change after the presentation. If it should play start to finish with nobody
presenting, make a [video](/workflows) instead: a deck can't be exported as one MP4 yet.

## How a good deck is built

- **One claim per slide**, with one visual that proves it.
- **Reveals** when the presenter should add an idea a piece at a time.
- **Branches and hotspots** for optional detail, so the main story still works if nobody opens them.
- **Speaker notes** that help the presenter instead of repeating the slide.

To change order, notes, reveals or branches by hand, use the [Slideshow panel](/studio/export#build-a-slideshow) in the browser
editor.

## Prompts that work

Ask for the deck's moving parts by name: "reveal the bullets one at a time", "branch to a detail slide from a hotspot", "add speaker notes". A full pitch deck in one prompt:

> Build a 5-slide pitch deck, 1920x1080. One idea per slide, each headline a complete-sentence claim (not a label), punchline first. Slide 2 reveals three pain points one at a time as fragments. Slide 3 shows bottom-up market math (accounts × ACV), not a bare "$40B TAM". Add presenter notes to every slide, and a hotspot on slide 3 that branches to a "sizing methodology" detail slide. I'll present it with `hyperframes present`.

This makes a deck you click through, not a video. Each reveal is a stop inside the slide; the hotspot opens a detail slide, and Back returns to the slide that held it.

## Present it

```bash
npx hyperframes present <project-directory>
```

Open the local link and press **P** (or click **Present**). The audience gets a clean full-screen view; you see the
live slide, your notes, what comes next and a timer. In Google Meet, share the audience **tab**; in Zoom, move that tab
into its own window and share the window.

Use **Next** to step through reveals, including those on the final slide. The Next button disappears once the
final slide's last reveal is shown.

<Warning>
  Do not run `hyperframes render` on a deck. It does not fail — a deck has no
  master root composition, so the render resolves only the **first** slide and
  writes a silently truncated MP4. You can get six seconds of a forty-second
  deck and no warning that anything is missing.

  Use `hyperframes present` for the live deck, or `hyperframes snapshot` for
  per-slide stills.
</Warning>
