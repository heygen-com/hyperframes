The HyperFrames Studio app for Mac is the easiest way to make a video with HyperFrames. You describe the video, an
agent on your computer builds it, and you change it by typing, pointing at the picture, drawing, or commenting.
It does not need a terminal.

## Download and install

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download">
    Go to [hyperframes.dev/studio](https://hyperframes.dev/studio) and choose **Download for macOS**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install">
    Open the downloaded file. The **Install HyperFrames** window copies the app into Applications and checks its
    signature. When it says **HyperFrames is in Applications.**, choose **Open HyperFrames**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your Mac's Applications folder is locked for your account, the installer puts the app in the Applications folder
inside your home folder instead.

## First launch

1. A short survey asks what you want to make and which agent you use. Every answer is optional, and **Skip all**
   counts as an answer. What you make decides where **Get inspired** opens. The agent you name starts your first
   chat if it is already set up.
2. Home opens with the **Create your first video** card. See
   [Make your first video](/studio-app/first-video).

A notice also explains what usage data the app records. Choose **Got it** to dismiss it.

## Sign in with HeyGen

You can look around without an account. The first time you send something, the app asks you to sign in with your
HeyGen account.

1. Choose **Continue with HeyGen**. Your browser opens hyperframes.dev.
2. Sign in there. The browser sends you back to the app, which shows **You're in!** and continues what you started.

Finish the browser sign-in within two minutes. If it expires, choose **Try again**. You can sign in or out at any
time from **Settings** > **Account**.
