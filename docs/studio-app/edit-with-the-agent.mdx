Every change starts the same way: show the agent where, say what, and send. You can type, point at something in
the picture, draw over it, or leave a comment at a moment in the video.

## Type a change

Type in the chat box, which says **Describe a change**, and press Enter. Type `@` to add moments, media, files, or
other projects to your message.

While the agent is working, Enter queues your message to run next. To change that, open **Settings** > **Agent**
and switch **While Framey works, Send** from **Queue** to **Steer**, which stops the run and starts again with your
words. Ctrl+Enter always does the other one.

## Point, draw, or comment on the picture

The tools above the picture:

| Tool | Key | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Select** | V | Click an element, then describe the change in the box that opens. Click again to pick what contains it. |
| **Draw** | D | Circle or mark a spot and say what should change. Several strokes become one drawing. |
| **Comment** | C | Pin a comment to a spot and moment: **What should change here?** |

The picture opens with **Draw**. Press Escape to go back to **Select**. Right-click an element for quick actions
such as **Ask Framey**, **Hide**, **Bring to front**, **Replace with**, and **Delete**.

You can also draw across the timeline to talk about a stretch of the video, such as "tighten this part".

## Send several edits at once

Picks, drawings, and comments do not run on their own. Each one becomes a row in **Pending video edits**, up to 10.
Check them, add a note for all of them, then send them as one request with the send button, for example
**Send 3 edits to Claude Code**. Choose **Edit note** to change a row's words, or **Delete** to drop it.

## Change the timeline yourself

Some changes are faster by hand. Hover a clip on the timeline to **Split** it at the playhead, **Delete** it, or
open **Quick edit** to change a video or audio clip's **Speed**, **Volume**, and **Fade**. Drag clips to move or trim
them.

Right-click an empty gap for **Close gap**, or to ask what should fill it. The app suggests ideas that fit what
plays around the gap.

## Get inspired

**Get inspired** is a library of short example moments from real videos: **Motion graphics**, **A-roll Cuts**,
and **Mobile Ads**. Find it on Home, in a new project's empty chat, and in the **Inspirations** tab beside your files.
Choose **Add** or **Use** to put a moment in your message as a reference, then say how to use it and send.

## Undo

Press Cmd+Z to undo and Cmd+Shift+Z to redo. Each agent run in the chat also has its own **Undo** button.
