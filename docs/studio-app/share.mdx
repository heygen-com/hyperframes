**Share** in the project's top bar turns your latest export into a review page and copies its link. Send it to
anyone who should watch or comment. When you change the video, the new version goes on the same link.

## Share a link

<HoverVideo className="hf-aside hf-spot" src="https://static.heygen.ai/hyperframes-oss/docs/studio-demos/share-button-v2.mp4" poster="/public/images/studio-app/spots/share-button-v2.jpg" hasAudio={false} label="Share in the top bar" />

Choose **Share**. The app uploads your last MP4 export, makes the review page and copies its link. If there's no
MP4 yet, it opens the Export panel first and shares as soon as the export finishes. See
[export a video](/studio-app/export).

Then HeyGen checks the video before reviewers can see it. The panel shows **Checking…**, which can take a few minutes.

You need to be signed in to HeyGen. The link is made in the workspace you're signed in to, named under **Shares to**;
choose **Change** to move it to another one.

## Choose who can open it

By default, **anyone in your workspace with the link can comment**. To change it, choose the row under **Who has
access**:

| Choice | Who can open it |
| --- | --- |
| **Only you** | Nobody else |
| **Only people you invite** | They sign in with the invited email |
| **Anyone in** your workspace | People signed in to that workspace, not people from outside |
| **Anyone with the link** | No sign-in. Guests type a name to comment |

Next to it, choose **can comment** or **can only watch**. Pick **Only you** before you share something that isn't ready
for others.

## Send a new version

When the project has changed since the version on the review page, the panel says so. Export an MP4, then choose
**Add this version**. It's the same link: everyone who has it sees the new version.
**Open review page** opens it in your browser. For a link anyone can open, the button is **View as reviewer** and
shows the page signed out, as your guests see it.

## Turn off the link

Choose **Turn off link**, then confirm. Everyone loses access at once, and the comments are kept. **Turn on with a new
link** shares it again under a new link.
