*Export, Change, 1080p, Render. The wait plays at 2×: this 12-second video took about 16 seconds on a MacBook.*

## This Mac or the cloud

Exports render on **This Mac** unless you pick the cloud. Under the render button, **Render in the cloud** opens
**Cloud rendering**, which compares the two and lets you **Add API key**. Once a key is saved, **Render on** in Export
picks **This Mac** or **Cloud**.

| | This Mac | Cloud |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost | Free | Paid per minute of video from your HeyGen API balance. Export shows the price before you start; 4K costs about twice as much as 1080p. |
| Your computer | Busy until it finishes | Free. Keep working while it renders. |
| Your files | Stay on your computer | The project is uploaded to HeyGen to render |
| Limits | Any shape and length | 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, at 24, 25, 30 or 60 fps, project up to 200 MB |

The key can also go in **Settings** > **HeyGen API key**. If the project is over 200 MB,
choose **Ask Framey to do it** under **Make it fit the cloud**: Framey moves your big videos and images to your
HeyGen assets. Either way, the video lands in your Downloads folder. A cloud render that has started can't be
stopped: **Cancel** stops waiting, but the cloud may still finish it and charge your balance.

## Choose the file

| Choice | Options | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Quality** | **1080p** or **Project size**, plus **4K** when the project fits it | 1080p |
| **Format** | **MP4**, **WebM**, **MOV** | MP4 |
| **Frame rate** | 24, 25, 30, 50, 60, or **Custom…** | 30 |

MP4 is the right choice for sharing and uploading. Pick another format only when the place you are sending it asks
for one.

## Render

Choose the render button, for example **Render 1080p MP4**. The panel shows **Preparing**, **Drawing frames**,
**Encoding the video**, **Assembling** and **Saving to Downloads**. You can close the panel and keep working; the render
continues. Choose **Cancel** to stop it.

**Export** is unavailable while the agent is building the project. Wait for the run to finish.

## Find the file

The video is saved to your **Downloads** folder, named after the project with the date and time, for example
`Spring Launch 2026-05-04 10.30.mp4`. When it is done, the panel shows **Export ready** with the file size:

- **Open video** plays it.
- **Show in Finder** (**Show in File Explorer** on Windows, **Show in folder** on Linux) opens the folder.

Watch the exported file once from start to end before you send it.

## If the export fails

- The render fails or does not start: choose the render button again. If it keeps failing,
  ask the agent to check the project, then export again.
- **The video was made but could not be saved to your Downloads folder**: free up space or check that the Downloads
  folder exists, then export again.
- **HyperFrames could not start its built-in FFmpeg**: the app may be damaged. Reinstall it from
  [hyperframes.studio/studio](https://hyperframes.studio/studio).
