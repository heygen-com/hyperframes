Found a video whose editing you love? Give it to the Studio app. Your video gets its cuts, captions and effects, with
your own people, words and brand in it. Showing a video once is faster than describing it, and a video you only half
like is still a better start than a blank page.

*Pick a reference from the feed: it's a person talking to camera, so the app goes to **Copy editing style**. Choose who's
on camera, then say what the video should talk about.*

Reference uses your HeyGen API key: the app watches the video through HeyGen, which uses your API balance. Add the
key in **Settings** > **HeyGen API key**, or when the app asks.

## 1. Pick the video

On Home, open **Reference**. Then:

- **Paste any URL**, then **Use link**. One public video from YouTube (a video or a Short), TikTok, Instagram or X, up to 30 minutes, or a
  published HyperFrames project.
- **Upload your reference**: drop a file from your computer.
- **Pick a reference** from the feed beside it, videos made to be remixed (also on Home as **Remix from inspiration**).

## 2. The app reads it and picks the way

The app watches the reference and decides how to remake it:

- **A person talking to camera** becomes **Copy editing style**: your video gets its cuts, captions and effects, with
  your footage or an AI avatar.
- **Graphics and animation with nobody on camera** becomes a **Video brief**: it recreates the motion with your topic
  and files.

If it can't tell, it asks you to choose between those two, then **Continue**.

## 3. Say what it's about

For **Copy editing style**, the app asks **Who's on camera?**

- **Upload your own takes.** Add **Editing instructions** if you want, for example "Cut the pauses, keep it under 30
  seconds".
- **An AI avatar:** your photo or a HeyGen presenter. Say what the avatar should say; a sentence or two is enough.

For a **Video brief**, write **What will your video talk about** (a sentence or two), and add your own pictures or
clips with **Upload assets**.

## 4. Swap what should be yours

**What to swap** lists the parts the app found in the reference, each with where it appears, for example "Your
opening question 0:00-0:02" or "Your website URL 0:02-0:05". Choose how to fill each:

| Choice | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Framey writes it** / **Framey decides** | Framey fills it to fit your topic |
| **Type yours** | Write the exact words |
| **Upload** | Use your own file |
| **Generate** | Make a picture or clip. Its price shows under the row once you pick it |

Choose a brand kit for the video, or **No brand kit**. Your workspace default stays as you set it. Then **Continue**;
Framey may ask a question or two about anything the video leaves open.

## 5. Compare with the original

While Framey builds, **Show original** at the top of the preview plays the reference over the picture. When the
build is done, the same button sets the two side by side, on one clock, so you can see what matches and what doesn't.
Then [edit the result](/studio-app/edit-with-the-agent) like any other video.

## Use other people's videos fairly

The app copies the editing style, not the footage: your video is built from your own takes, avatar, words and
media. Don't reuse the original's footage, music or logos unless you have the rights to them.
