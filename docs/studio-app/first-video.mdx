The **Create your first video** card on Home builds a launch video from four parts, each recreated from a launch
video that shipped. You pick the parts, add your website or an idea, and the agent builds one video from them.

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Hook** | Grab attention in the first seconds |
| **Proof** | Show the product doing its job |
| **Turn** | Flip from problem to answer |
| **Call to action** | Land the brand and the ask |

## Build it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the four parts">
    The card starts with one clip in each part. Choose **Shuffle all four** for a new set, or hover a part to
    **Shuffle** it or browse the others.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your website or idea">
    Type your website, such as `yourcompany.com`, or describe your idea. A website gives the agent your fonts, colors
    and logo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Use this cut">
    The request goes straight to the agent. If you are not signed in or have no agent yet, the app walks you through
    that first, then sends it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What should happen

The app creates a project and opens it. While the agent works, the chat shows what it is doing, such as **Reading
files** or **Editing files**. On your first video, a short tour shows you around while it builds.

When the agent finishes, the video is ready to play, change, and export.

## Start from your own words instead

You do not need the card. Type what you want in Home's prompt box and press Enter. Type `@` to add files, other
projects, or media. To open the card again later, choose **Take the step by step** next to **Get inspired**.
