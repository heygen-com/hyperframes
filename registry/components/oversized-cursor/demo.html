Offline mode, one year in

Priya Raman · 6 min read

A year ago we shipped offline mode to every workspace. Since then, 38% of edits have happened on a plane, a train, or a flaky café connection.

Most of that traffic lands in the first ten minutes after a device reconnects, so this post walks through how the sync queue drains without locking the editor.
